| brix.ch8 | Brix | Andreas Gustafsson, 1990 |

| invaders.ch8 | Space Invaders | David Winter |
| ufo.ch8 | UFO | Lutz V, 1992 |
| wipeoff.ch8 | Wipe Off | Joseph Weisbecker |
